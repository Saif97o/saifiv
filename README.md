## About Me

I'm a Graphic Designer with a strong interest in visual communication, branding,
advertising design, print production, and digital creativity.

My work combines **creative design with practical production knowledge**, allowing
me to take projects from the initial concept through design, preparation, printing,
and final production.

I particularly enjoy working on:

- 🎨 Brand Identity & Visual Systems
- 🖨️ Print & Production Design
- 📢 Advertising & Promotional Materials
- 📚 Book & Educational Material Design
- 💻 Digital Content & Creative Projects
- 📦 Packaging & Product Presentation
- ✨ Mockups & Commercial Visuals
